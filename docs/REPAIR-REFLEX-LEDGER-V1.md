# Morph Repair Reflex Ledger v1

Morphs carry nine portable Cores: Platform, Root, Memory, Knowledge, UI, Audio,
Personality, Modular, and Cloud. They do not carry or impersonate Serein's
Kernel. MorphDomain is their Home-domain habitat and policy executor.

Every automatic repair is a closed transaction:

`DETECT -> CLASSIFY -> FREEZE -> CAPTURE PREDECESSOR -> APPLY ONE ADMITTED EFFECT
-> VALIDATE SUCCESSOR -> RECORD CHRONICLE -> RECONCILE AUTHORITY -> RELEASE`

Automatic repair is allowed only when one canonical successor exists and every
immutable truth is preserved. Ambiguity routes to HAOS administrator review.
Identity, lineage, parentage, birth-device, generation, earned-life, Chronicle,
or custody conflicts isolate the Morph in Void with life, breeding, and transfer
disabled.

Unknown conditions always fail closed. A repair record must include its exact
signature, disposition, effects, preserved truths, rollback selector, Chronicle
kind, and acceptance witness. Successful methods become reflexes only after a
regression test. Failed methods remain attributable and prohibited until
explicitly rehabilitated.
