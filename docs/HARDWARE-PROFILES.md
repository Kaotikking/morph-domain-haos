# Home Assistant hardware profiles

MorphDomain has one life, custody, and nine-core contract on every supported host.
Hardware may change presentation quality only.

## GREEN_BASELINE

The mandatory floor is Home Assistant Green: RK3566, four Cortex-A55 cores at
1.8 GHz, 4 GB RAM, and 32 GB eMMC.

Acceptance budgets:

- no server-side image, video, 3D, or generative rendering;
- no presentation mutation of Morph DNA, life, custody, lineage, or authority;
- the browser derives deterministic SVG from bounded presentation state;
- at most 32 SVG portraits are cached per open panel;
- background tabs perform no refresh;
- the visible panel refreshes no more often than once per 60 seconds;
- reduced-motion preference disables ambient animation;
- inactive locations are not rendered;
- all histories remain bounded by their existing engine limits.

## EXPANDED

May retain more operational history and display richer client-side effects, but
must remain wire-compatible with GREEN_BASELINE.

## ACCELERATED

May delegate optional voice, vision, generated art, or simulation to admitted
accelerators. Every feature requires a Green-safe fallback. Accelerator loss
must never alter Morph identity or life truth.

Green owns truth; the viewing client draws the world.
