# Morph Domain pre-battle reflex gate

Battle logic is not admitted until all nine foundation reflexes remain proven.

1. **Code Haven admission** preserves prior place and digest before entering the only Morph-state, DNA, and presentation write boundary.
2. **Code Haven discharge** requires the matching open admission receipt and exits through Horizon.
3. **Nursery pair admission** preflights both Morphs, moves neither on failure, admits both together, and starts a five-second compatibility window.
4. **Hatch and graduation** keeps an egg sealed for the Nursery clock, assigns its deterministic elemental pool name at hatch, and graduates it to Horizon after 72 hours.
5. **Transfer recovery** reconciles interrupted or expired operations without inventing identity, custody, or generation.
6. **Deploy to admitted frame** uses the destination-owned durable return identifier and the full prepare, inactive-stage, commit, release, activate, and reconcile sequence.
7. **Recall from admitted frame** freezes the exact current custody revision, imports its current portable state, commits HAOS custody, and places the Morph in Horizon.
8. **Void stasis and withdrawal** binds an exact snapshot digest, stops time, enforces the 24-hour lock, and releases only to Horizon or a receipt-backed Code Haven admission.
9. **Environmental life interaction** is an autonomous Horizon/Gardens event worth +1. Operator care is a distinct event worth +2.

## Living Reflex expansion

Reflex 9 includes the neutral Morph life-call loop. A need or intention may
open a bounded call, but the call is not an Operator demand and has no implicit
notification channel. Horizon may satisfy it through the environment, another
Morph may answer it, or an Operator may respond through an admitted surface.
Every resolution records who or what answered. Device-specific policy controls
presentation.

Morph Window is the paired consumer contract: a dedicated frame can display a
trusted pixel-avatar snapshot and reduced scene while HAOS retains custody.
It does not add a tenth reflex or duplicate the Morph.

## Invariants

- Morph lineage generation never doubles as a transfer counter; transport uses custody revision.
- Public reads do not advance life or reconcile transactions.
- Every mutation is administrator-authorized, idempotent, durably stored, and API-readable.
- Generic place movement does not substitute for Code Haven, Void, Nursery-pair, frame, or recovery reflexes.
- Battle remains `BLOCKED_UNTIL_REFLEXES_1_9_PROVEN`.
