# Morph World Presentation Foundation v1

This contract turns DNAv1 truth into a stable, device-agnostic presentation without changing Morph identity, custody, life, or battle state.

## Gen-1 registry

Element IDs are immutable: `01 FIRE`, `02 AIR`, `03 EARTH`, `04 WATER`. Private Serein Founders are `F-01 Ember`, `F-02 Breeze`, `F-03 Sentinel`, and `F-04 Pulse`. Public primitive templates use `E-01..E-04`. A first platform starter uses `L1-01..L1-04`. A Founder-descendant Legendary uses `L2-<sorted element vector>`; Dustdevil is Gen-2 `L2-02.03`, not Gen-1. Names and localization never change identity.

## Twin-world onboarding

Android and HAOS are complementary presentations of one contract. Android is mobile, outward, social, and exploratory. HAOS is persistent, environmental, and archival. The owning platform runs life locally and independently; transfer remains optional and one active owner is mandatory.

The first Morph selected on either platform is Legendary. A valid import suppresses starter creation. Living with that Legendary in Horizon may unlock knowledge of the other three primitive lineages, but discovery never creates another Morph or Legendary.

## Morph ID Card

`serein.morph-id-card.v1` is the smallest complete SERN-readable Morph representation. Its nine required cores are Identity, Lineage, Life, Expression, Capability, Relationship, Memory, Presentation, and Authority. The full canonical record is used for transfer and reconciliation; a compact digest projection is used for discovery and attribution.

## Care and expression

Care is `CARE|SUS:n|ATT:n|PLY:n|SOC:n|AFF:n`, each `0..5`: sustenance, timely attention, varied play, reciprocal/directional social care, and lineage/individual-aligned affection. Charging supplies sustenance only. Age permits development; it does not award it.

The resolver is `DNA + LINEAGE + EARNED_CARE_VECTOR + CARE_EVIDENCE + DIRECTIONAL_RELATIONSHIPS + MASTERY = ELIGIBLE_STAGE + ELIGIBLE_BRANCHES`. The five care paths are RESTORATIVE, ATTUNED, EXPLORATORY, RELATIONAL, and GUARDIAN. Mastery is earned and never regresses. A neglected Morph is never permanently trapped: sustained later care may reach the same culmination eligibility, while Chronicle preserves its distinct journey.

## Capability and visible form

The five capability paths are MOVEMENT, PROTECTION, RESTORATION, PERCEPTION, and PROJECTION. Capability growth changes visible expression. Every form reserves these layers: `LINEAGE_BASE + CARE_BRANCH_EXPRESSION + POWER/CAPABILITY_EXPRESSION + TRAIT_MODIFIER + ORIGIN_SIGNATURE + CURRENT_CONTEXT`.

## Human presentation

All frames use the same concise ritual: `IDENTIFY -> CALL -> FORM -> REVEAL -> SIGNATURE -> SETTLE`. The player-facing vocabulary stays small: identity call, greeting ritual, expression call, up to four ordinary actions, and one signature action. Localization changes labels, rhythm, and call patterns—not SERN IDs or mechanics. Word Pool A names identity, Pool B expression and culmination, and Pool C action and voice; pools are multilingual and append-only.

## Acceptance lock

The executable fixtures prove all four elements, charging-only limits, recovered care, equal-care capability divergence, Founder signature reservation, constrained-frame fallback, import suppression, Horizon discovery without creation, fail-closed schema/ID behavior, and one active owner. Battle mechanics are outside this foundation.
