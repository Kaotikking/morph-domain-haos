# MorphDomain battle beta v1

The first admitted battle mode is deterministic **unranked sparring** inside
Horizon or Serein Gardens. It consumes the existing nine-core snapshot; it does
not create a second Morph model or transfer path.

- Two distinct, hatched, nine-core Morphs must both be under HAOS custody.
- Move slots are STATUS, SPEED, POWER, and SUPER. Names come from the existing
  elemental lexicon while machine identity remains SERN data.
- Care, expression, elemental relationship, and bounded trait stage influence
  the result. UNKNOWN required facts fail closed under UMP.
- The receipt is deterministic and replay-safe. Battle changes no DNA, custody,
  rank, injury, evolution, care, or snapshot digest.
- Ranked play, injury, rewards, evolution unlocks, frame armor, combined forms,
  remote battles, and automatic battle selection remain inactive.

This is a beta foundation: a real resolver and durable local receipt without
prematurely committing later battle progression rules.
